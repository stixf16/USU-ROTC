# USU + Air Force ROTC — A Starter Guide

A short, straight-talk guide for a student headed to **Utah State University** who is thinking about joining the **Air Force**. It lays out the recommended path (Air Force ROTC at USU, Detachment 860), who to call, and answers to the most common first questions — scholarships, time commitment, how the program works, and the service commitment after commissioning.

Prepared by **Col (Ret.) Jason "Stix" Evenson, USAF** (F-16, MQ-9, T-38).

## Quick facts covered

- **The move:** Do Air Force ROTC at USU — graduate in ~4 years with your degree *and* a commission as an officer.
- **First step:** Call USU AFROTC Detachment 860 (recruiting: (435) 797-4120, det860@usu.edu).
- **Scholarships:** What's covered, and how to compete for an in-college scholarship.
- **Commitment:** No obligation the first two years (non-scholarship); service commitment after commissioning is 4 years for most jobs (longer for flying).
